# Musical RPG

Shared language for a fantasy role-playing game whose inhabitants express
events and intentions through musical theatre.

## Language

**Musical scene**:
An in-world song-and-dance performance shaped by the characters and
events in the game world.

**Player intention**:
The in-character aim the player chooses to express during a musical scene.

**Sung contribution**:
A participant's vocal expression within a musical scene. A player's sung
contribution is generated by the game to express their chosen player intention.
