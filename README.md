# Musical RPG

An experimental fantasy role-playing game where characters turn events
in their world into musical theatre. Players can join generated musical
scenes and influence what happens next.

Players choose an in-character intention, which the game expresses through
a sung contribution that can affect the outcome. They remain free to move,
interrupt or leave, with consequences appropriate to the situation.

The inhabitants treat singing with complete sincerity, as a normal part
of life. The comedy comes from earnest motives, extravagant performances
and emergent mishaps, with room for unexpectedly touching moments.

The project explores both the appeal of a musical RPG and how much
GPT-6 Astra can realise from a loosely specified brief. A human producer
sets the creative aim, tone and practical limits. Astra develops the
creative and technical details through a build-run-observe-revise cycle,
with the producer judging playable results and directing each iteration.

## Status

The initial concept exploration is complete. We are qualifying development
tools and music-generation dependencies before the first playable musical
encounter; there is no playable implementation yet.
